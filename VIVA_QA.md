# Viva Preparation — Quick Answers

### What is the project?
It is an exploratory data-analysis project on Zomato restaurant data using Python.

### Why did you choose this dataset?
It contains restaurant information such as city, cuisine, price range, ratings, votes, online delivery, and table booking, so it is suitable for learning data cleaning, visualization, and exploratory analysis.

### Which libraries did you use?
Pandas and NumPy for data handling, Matplotlib and Seaborn for visualization, and Jupyter/Google Colab for running the notebook.

### How many records are there?
The dataset contains 9,551 restaurant records and 21 columns.

### What did you do with rating 0?
A rating of 0 is treated as not rated. Rating-based analysis excludes these records rather than interpreting 0 as a zero-star customer rating.

### Which city has the most restaurants?
New Delhi, with 5,473 records.

### Which cuisine appears most often?
North Indian, with 3,960 cuisine mentions.

### What is the relationship between votes and rating?
There is a moderate positive association, with a correlation of about 0.41 among rated restaurants.

### Does higher cost cause higher ratings?
No. The cost-rating correlation is very weak (about 0.08), and correlation does not establish causation.

### What did you learn from the project?
We learned how to inspect and clean real-world data, handle missing values, choose appropriate visualizations, calculate summary statistics and correlations, and communicate findings clearly.
